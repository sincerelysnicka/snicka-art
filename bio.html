artist biography

Photo courtesy of Sarah Koscielniak

snicka (b. 1989 in St. Louis, MO) is a printmaker, painter, and photographer working in Grand Rapids, MI. She was drawn to art in middle and high school by the hand-drawn animation of transgressive late-night cartoons that aired long past bedtime.

While earning her BA in Printmaking from Grand Valley State University, snicka worked at a thrift store, elbow-deep in other people's castoffs, deciding what still had life left in it. The job left her with a lasting interest in decay, consumption, and waste—an interest that has since drifted toward a related question: what counts as success, and how much time do we waste chasing it? A recurring motif of men's button-down shirts and ties can be traced back to a high school exchange trip to Berlin, where she was struck (and quite delighted) by how many people wore suits. Artists who influence her work include Ben Shahn, Yoshitomo Nara, and William Kentridge.

snicka has been part of the artist collective TITANS since 2025 and volunteers with the local nonprofit Avenue for the Arts. Outside of her studio, she can be found gardening, sewing, or trying to win at Call of Duty and poker.
